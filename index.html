Our Thesis

Infrastructure first.
Always.

The most transformative technology companies in Africa won't be built by copying Silicon Valley. They'll be built by people who understand that the continent's greatest leverage points are infrastructure problems — and that solving them requires hardware, software, and deep local knowledge working together.

01
Hardware-first thinking
The most defensible systems are those anchored in the physical world. Software that floats above the ground can be copied. Hardware that reads ground truth cannot.
02
Data as infrastructure
Africa has a data collection gap. Industries that have operated without reliable data for decades represent extraordinary opportunities for the first platform that gets the collection layer right.
03
Networks that compound
We build systems where value grows with every node added. A single sensor is useful. A network of sensors across an industry becomes an intelligence layer that no competitor can easily replicate.

Certa
Industrial Fuel Intelligence
IoT-based fuel monitoring for industrial and retail operators across Africa. Certa installs independent hardware sensors on fuel tanks and dispensers, creating verifiable ground truth data that no operator can manipulate — solving the fraud problem that existing software solutions fundamentally cannot.
